# O3 AIY state-pattern rescue — focused novelty audit

**Record ID:** `O3_AIY_STATE_RESCUE_NOVELTY_AUDIT_V1`  
**Disposition:** focused literature audit complete; prospective study design narrowed. No biological outcomes were collected.

## Result

The broad claim that motor state gates sensory processing is already supported in *C. elegans*. Ji et al. (2021) showed RIM-dependent AIY motor-state representation, sensory gating, and reduced forward-run persistence after RIM ablation. Kumar et al. (2023) showed motor-context gating in mechanosensory processing. Kocabas et al. (2012) established that AIY activity patterns can drive locomotory behavior, so generic AIY excitation cannot identify a state-code effect. Dunn et al. (2025) added a recent whole-brain, closed-loop account of state-dependent sensory timing and short-term memory. Cell-specific SER-2 rescue in AIY is also known in a distinct olfactory-imprinting paradigm (Jin et al., 2016).

The plausible contribution is narrower: test whether restoring the forward-state timing of AIY activity under RIM perturbation rescues thermotaxis-related forward-run persistence and state-dependent thermosensory representation better than a time-yoked pattern matched for stimulation dose. A bounded literature search did not find this exact contrast; it does not establish a “first” claim.

## Analysis and next action

The next project action is to execute a prospective worm study with same-animal, time-aligned behavior, temperature, and AIY activity. The causal comparison is state-contingent replay versus a photon-dose and waveform-matched yoked replay in the RIM-perturbed background. An intact group defines the reference pattern; opsin-negative illumination controls assess optical artifacts. The worm remains the independent unit. A blinded instrument/target-engagement pilot must determine workable timing, artifacts, and animal-level variance before a confirmatory cohort can be meaningfully sized.

The AI-side M2 reliability-context experiment remains an exploratory synthetic boundary result. Its reliability mapping is not established by the worm literature and should not carry the main biological-to-AI claim. A separate artificial state-gated update can proceed in parallel, with task-matched recurrent and Bayesian references, but remains conditional until the biological computation is directly tested.
