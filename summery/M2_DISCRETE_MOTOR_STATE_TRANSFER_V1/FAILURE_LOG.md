# Failure and boundary log

1. The previous continuous 2D transfer gave the model a vector-valued actuator velocity, broader than the discrete locomotor-state representation directly supported by the biological evidence. V1 here narrows the feedback channel to the sign of realized velocity; it still uses an artificial target-tracking task.
2. The observed primary advantage is specific to comparison with the preceding command sign. It does not beat the zero-input control, and the recipient-yoke interval crosses zero. Do not summarize the result as proof of a self-contingency mechanism.
3. The fixed reactive policy beats all learned arms on the fast-target profile. This profile-specific weakness limits any broad transfer claim even though the primary long-burst task is learnable and the self-state arm beats the reactive policy there.
4. The six-seed preflight was inspected before the full study and showed mixed learned-arm ordering. It is disclosed, uses separate seeds, and makes the full study exploratory rather than confirmatory.
5. This study does not instantiate the worm thermotaxis task, use measured AIY signals, or reproduce a known biological circuit equation. It cannot support biological causation or an NMI-level general AI claim.
