## Reading statuses

- **Historical/registered** does not mean positive or ready to execute.
- **Frozen** means the specified artifact is fixed; it does not mean the hypothesis passed.
- A control listed as **proposed** is not part of a preregistered experiment and cannot be executed before its own freeze.
- M1's four arms are immutable for the existing RR19 primary test. Project-level capacity/topology contrasts require a separately frozen experiment and cannot be retrofitted after inspecting M1 outcomes.
- No row combines biological neurons, graph edges, or functional traces across datasets.
