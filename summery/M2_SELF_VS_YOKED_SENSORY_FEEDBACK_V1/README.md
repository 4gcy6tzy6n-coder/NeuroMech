Primary result: at 20-s gradient reversals, self-contingent sensory-site feedback exceeds its exact-instantaneous-marginal cross-agent yoke by +0.04618 model-distance units/s (95% seed-block bootstrap interval +0.04252 to +0.04994; 100/100 blocks positive). At the 5-s schedule the small contrast reverses. These are retrospective source-model simulation results, not biological validation or AI-transfer evidence.
