# Failure and limitation log — M2 AIY premotor-state prediction V2

- V1's strict all-known-label target could not score any RIM-ablated positive cases. Its result remains recorded separately; V2 changes the target semantics and is post-result exploratory.
- The 2-second endpoint has only 6–18 positive samples per animal; one or two events can move per-animal AUC materially.
- Only five animals per group are public in these selected tables. Bootstrap and exact permutation values are descriptive and do not cure small-cohort uncertainty or establish randomized group exchangeability.
- AVA's larger WT-minus-RIM incremental-AUC contrast argues against an AIY-specific interpretation. The analysis did not test causal precedence, direction-specific neural pathways, or mediation.
- Ten-fold within-animal blocked prediction does not constitute held-out-animal generalization. The current signal may reflect shared premotor dynamics or measurement timing.
- No behavior outcome after intervention, AI model, or novel wet-lab cohort was evaluated.
