**Fig. M2-Burst V1 | Sensory-site motor feedback improves tracking over small controls, with no resolved advantage over GRU-8.** **a,** Held-out mean squared tracking error against expected missing-run length under 50% expected missingness. Points are means across 24 training-seed blocks; intervals are 95% percentile bootstrap intervals over seed-level means, with 128 held-out episodes nested in each seed and condition. **b,** Long-burst paired differences, baseline MSE minus sensory-site MSE; positive values favor sensory-site feedback. Points and 95% intervals use 10,000 crossed bootstrap resamples of 24 training seeds and paired episode IDs (128 episodes per seed). The output-site and four-parameter generic scalar-RNN contrasts are positive; the GRU-8 interval crosses zero. The small policies each have four trainable parameters; GRU-8 has 321. MSE, mean squared tracking error. All observations come from the artificial target-tracking simulator and do not constitute biological validation.
