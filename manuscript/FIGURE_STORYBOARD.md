Schematic: biological observation → source-supported operation → artificial implementation → task outcome. Use solid arrows for evidence in hand and dashed arrows for hypotheses. Display the information/task/control correspondence checklist. Do not illustrate the framework as a validated law.

## Figure 4 — Cross-case synthesis and prospective discriminator

Matrix rows: biological variable, functional observable, artificial input, preserved operation, matched controls, outcome scope. Emphasize that correspondence is a hypothesis-generating audit, not a necessary/sufficient law established by these cases.

Add only after canonical completion. Plot the frozen primary contrast and all three conditions, event-revealed information control, generic GRU pair and task-aware particle filter. If missing or invalid, omit the figure and retain an explicit Results boundary; do not invent a favorable panel.
